Spells and status

  • FireBurns most enemies away. 22 mana.
  • IceFreezes an enemy for five seconds. Hit it again to shatter it. 26 mana.
  • ZapChains through up to three nearby enemies. 34 mana.
  • BurningFrom lava and orange skulls. Water puts it out.
  • FrozenFrom icy pools and blue skulls. You move and jump slower.
  • HexedFrom ghosts and eye orbs. No spells for three seconds.
  • HasteThe green potion. Faster and higher.
  • ElectrifiedThe storm globe. Invulnerable, free spells, touch to zap.

The hero is Biscuit, a blue hamster wizard drawn in code, chosen by the kids. Status effects and deaths wrap around his outline. Enemies, spells, status overlays, deaths, weather and pickups are pixelfx effects; areas reuse the same effects with different palettes. Skies are drawn in code.

Art credits: tiles, trees, props and pine backdrops from Legacy Fantasy: High Forest by Anokolisa, recolored per area. Haunted Grove backdrop: original artwork made for Lantern Run. Effects from pixelfx by Patch (MIT).
